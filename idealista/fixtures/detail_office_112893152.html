A magnificent office is offered for sale in the Simancas district, Madrid, located in an office building with an elevator, freight elevator, loading and unloading dock, and 24-hour security service.

The property is distributed over two floors and features 18 private offices, 35 independent workstations, a boardroom, a kitchen, and two bathrooms. It is equipped with hot/cold air conditioning, raised flooring, Climalit exterior carpentry, and a security door.

The office is in very good condition, offering an excellent opportunity for companies looking for a spacious and functional space to carry out their professional activities.

Additionally, there is the possibility of purchasing parking spaces starting at €22,000 or renting them from €90/month.

An excellent opportunity to establish or expand your business in a business area of Madrid.

Simancas is one of the areas with the greatest projection and transformation in eastern Madrid. Located next to the prestigious Arturo Soria area and just a few minutes from the city center, it combines an excellent location, a complete range of services, and magnificent transport links, making it an increasingly sought-after option for both living and investing.

The neighborhood stands out for its urban and residential character, as well as its proximity to the renovated Julián Camarillo business district, known as the "Madrid Silicon Valley," where numerous technology companies, innovation centers, and top-tier offices are concentrated. This evolution has significantly boosted the quality of life and the appeal of the area, which offers a wide range of commercial, gastronomic, and service options.

In its surroundings, we find important green spaces and recreational areas such as El Paraíso Park, Quinta de los Molinos Park—one of Madrid's most iconic spaces due to the blooming of its almond trees—and the nearby Juan Carlos I Park, all ideal for walking, playing sports, and enjoying leisure time outdoors.

The area has an excellent educational offering thanks to the proximity of some of the most prestigious centers in Madrid, including Colegio Arturo Soria, Lycée Français, Colegio Nuestra Señora de Santa María, Colegio San José del Parque (Maristas), Colegio Ramón y Cajal, Colegio Brains, International School of Madrid, and ESIC Business & Marketing School, among others.

Healthcare coverage is guaranteed by the proximity of reference hospitals and medical centers such as the Ramón y Cajal University Hospital, La Paz University Hospital, Vithas Madrid Arturo Soria University Hospital, HM Nuevo Belén University Hospital, and various public and private health centers.

A few minutes away are some of Madrid's main shopping and leisure areas, such as Arturo Soria Plaza, Alcalá Norte, Palacio de Hielo, El Corte Inglés, and Hipercor at Campo de las Naciones, in addition to the IFEMA exhibition center and a wide range of restaurants and local shops.

Transport links are one of Simancas' great strengths. The Simancas, García Noblejas, Suanzes, and Ciudad Lineal metro stations allow quick access to the center of Madrid and the main business areas. Likewise, the immediate connection to the M-30, M-40, A-2, and M-11 facilitates travel both inside and outside the city. The proximity to the Adolfo Suárez Madrid-Barajas International Airport and the Chamartín station adds significant strategic value to the location.

A magnificent opportunity to enjoy an area in full evolution, perfectly connected and close to the prestigious Arturo Soria environment, where comfort, services, and quality of life combine to offer an excellent residential experience.
