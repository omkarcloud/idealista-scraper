Walter Haus Real Estate presents this EXCLUSIVE presentation of this brand-new, fully renovated 286 m² home located in a landmark building dating from 1953, combining classic elegance and modernity. The property stands out for its high ceilings, original wooden floors, and large windows that provide abundant natural light.

Its layout is ideal for both a family and an investment, with a design that perfectly balances the social and private spaces.

The home features a spacious living-dining room, a library, an office, and three bedrooms. The master bedrooms each have an en-suite bathroom, and there is a guest toilet and another bathroom. The modern, open-plan kitchen features a breakfast bar and a practical dining table.

Located in a well-connected area, this property is surrounded by all amenities, including metro and bus stops. The south-facing bedrooms overlook a large courtyard, ensuring tranquility and natural light.

Purchasing this property means investing in a home filled with light and style, with all the necessary infrastructure for a comfortable life. Walter Haus guarantees an unparalleled buying experience.

Walter Haus Real Estate is a real estate group with offices in major Spanish capitals, in prime locations such as Madrid, Barcelona, and the Costa Brava.

We have over 20 years of experience, a dynamic team of professionals with experience in personal advice, ongoing training, and highly specialized expertise in each area of operation, allowing us to find solutions to each client's needs.
