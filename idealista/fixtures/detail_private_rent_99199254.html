Second floor without elevator of 36 m². Located in a very quiet street in Barceloneta. It consists of a living room with a kitchenette, 1 double bedroom, and another room with a double sofa bed. Full bathroom with a shower tray. All rooms are exterior with lots of light. It has a balcony with a side view of the sea. Heating with electric radiators and air conditioning. 50 meters from the beach.

Rental Conditions:
• Rent: €1,114 / month
• Deposit: 1 month's rent (€1,114) plus the current month's rent upfront.
